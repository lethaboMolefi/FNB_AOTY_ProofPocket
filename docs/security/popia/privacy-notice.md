# Privacy notice (version 2026-10-25, draft for the Information Officer)

This is the source text for the in-app notice at `/privacy-notice` (`apps/web/src/features/public/PrivacyNotice.tsx`). When it changes, change `PRIVACY_NOTICE_VERSION` in `packages/shared/src/constants.ts` too. Founders who joined under an older version are then asked to agree again (a follow-up task listed in `not-implemented.md`).

**Responsible party:** ProofPocket (Team KamosoByte). **Information Officer:** [to be appointed and registered; see `processing-register.md`].

## Who we are
ProofPocket keeps one shared business record for founders, their incubators and funders. We never hold or move money.

## What we collect
- Your name, email address and business details (name, sector, town, registration number).
- Documents you upload to be checked: company registration, owner ID, bank confirmation, proof of address, and others you choose.
- Your books: money in and out, stock, suppliers, payment requests, and proofs of payment your clients send.
- Your saved payment details. Account numbers are encrypted; others only ever see the last four digits.
- Sign-in records and an audit log of important actions, to keep your account and your money safe.

**Your clients:** when a client opens your payment link and sends a proof of payment, we keep the proof and the reference so you can confirm the money arrived.

## Why we use it (lawful basis, POPIA s11)
- To check your documents and show you as Verified: your consent and our agreement with you.
- To work out your readiness score and show your progress: our agreement with you.
- To let your incubator support you, and verified funders see only what you allow: your consent, which you can change at any time under Privacy & sharing.
- To spot fraud (reused proofs, changed supplier bank details, scam messages): our legitimate interest and yours in preventing fraud.

## Who can see it
- Your incubator's advisers, if you choose an incubator.
- Verified funders, only as much as you allow (nothing, a summary, or full books). Applying to a programme shares your profile with that funder only.
- ProofPocket's admin checkers, to check documents and handle reports.
- No one else. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

## Where it's kept
In Google Cloud in Johannesburg (africa-south1). Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase, and email is sent through Gmail; these may process your email address outside South Africa under Google's data protection terms (POPIA s72). See `cross-border-register.md`.

## How long we keep it
- Business records (books, documents, payments) for 5 years after your last activity, as South African tax law requires.
- Unfinished uploads are deleted after a day. The audit log is kept for 5 years.

## Your rights
- See and download everything we keep about your business: Privacy & sharing → Your data → Download my data.
- Ask us to correct or delete your data from the same place. We reply within 30 days. Some records must be kept by law; we'll tell you which.
- Object to how we use your data, or complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za, complaints.IR@inforegulator.org.za).
